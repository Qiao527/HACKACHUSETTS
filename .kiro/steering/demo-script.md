# Demo Video Script (invoke with /demo-script)

Hard limit: under 3:00 (target 2:45). Public on YouTube. No copyrighted music, no third-party trademarks. Must show the product running. Disclose synthetic inputs on screen.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | "The owner pays for the panels. The tenants keep the savings. Why would the owner proceed?" Show the building (public facts) and the synthetic label. |
| 0:20–0:55 | Drag the tenant-share slider: owner NPV and tenant savings trade off, kWh stays the same, the mutual-benefit zone lights up (or honestly does not exist). |
| 0:55–1:25 | Open the cited roof finding (page + quote), confirm the replacement timing, and show how roof costs move the zone. |
| 1:25–2:05 | AI coordinator proposes a range inside the engine's zone; show owner and tenant explanations; switch to Spanish. |
| 2:05–2:25 | Print the brief: both outcomes, lease/meter/utility questions, "eligibility not verified". |
| 2:25–2:45 | Kiro: a spec, an Analyze Requirements finding, a property-test counterexample and its fix, a hook firing. Closing line. |

Closing line: "SolarAlign shows why a sunny roof is not enough: the project has to fit the roof's lifecycle and the people who pay and the people who benefit."

Wording rules: "flags" not "certifies"; "may change the result" for incentives; never "fines saved".
